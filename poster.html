The World Today

TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Huang returns to Nvidia and markets erupt, Ukraine sinks a Russian corvette in Libya, Brussels unveils a 22nd sanctions package, and Tehran bills Washington $290 billion.

Tech · Markets

HUANG RETURNS TO NVIDIA

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, 11 days after he resigned in scandal. The shares leapt 31% and the Nasdaq jumped 6.5% in one of Wall Street's wildest days. Trump backed the return; lawmakers attacked it, and FBI, SEC and antitrust probes still hang over the firm.

War · Libya

UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN CORVETTE IN LIBYA

Ukraine says seven of its sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base, destroying a Ka-27 helicopter aboard and hitting the arms carrier MV Angara — 2,400 km from the front line. Satellite images show one of Russia's newest warships submerged at its berth.

Europe · Brussels

EU UNVEILS 22ND SANCTIONS PACKAGE

Ursula von der Leyen proposed the EU's 22nd sanctions package on Monday: some 60 more Russian banks cut from SWIFT, a crypto sanctions task force, 70 more shadow-fleet tankers listed, and energy bans extended to electricity. Russian ex-combatants face a visa ban, and Russian teams are barred from EU events under their flag.

Gulf · Tehran

IRAN ORDERS US TO PAY $290 BILLION

Iranian courts have awarded $290 billion in damages against the United States for the war and decades of hostile acts, a judiciary official said on Monday. Tehran has also sued Gulf Arab states that hosted US forces, and vows to break the American naval blockade “by all available means”.

Briefs

• Russian jets entered South Korea's air defence zone off Ulleung Island on Tuesday; residents were told to shelter, then the alert was lifted within minutes. Seoul says its airspace was not violated.

• Kim Yo Jong says North Korea may shut Switzerland's Pyongyang office after Bern sanctioned a children's camp over transfers of Ukrainian children.

• Iran will ration electricity with daily power cuts as fuel shortages bite ahead of winter.
